How to use RecipePress

1. Add your recipes

Click ＋ New recipe and type the name, the ingredients (one per line) and the steps (one per line). Start each ingredient with its amount, like “1 1/2 cups flour” – then RecipePress can make half, double or triple the recipe for you. A line that ends with “:” (for example “For the sauce:”) becomes a small heading.

Found a recipe on a website or in an email? Copy it and click 📋 Paste a recipe (or just press Ctrl+V). RecipePress finds the name, ingredients and steps. Check them and add a photo.

Everything is saved automatically on this PC.

2. Print

Open 👁 Print preview, choose a layout (full page, a 4 × 6 or 5 × 7 recipe card, or two cards on one sheet with cut lines) and a design. Then click 🖨 Print (Ctrl+P). For a PDF, choose “Save as PDF” or “Microsoft Print to PDF” as the printer.

Tip: for recipe cards, print on card stock and set the printer's paper size to the card size, or use “Two 4 × 6 cards on Letter paper” and cut along the dashed lines.

3. A whole family cookbook (Pro)

Fill in the title, subtitle and dedication on the right and click 📖 Print cookbook. You get a cover page with a photo, a dedication page, contents with page numbers, a chapter page for every category and all your recipes. Print it at home or save a PDF and have it printed and bound at a copy shop – a lovely gift.

Keep your recipes safe

Use ☰ More → Back up all recipes now and then and keep the file somewhere safe (a USB stick or your cloud drive). Restore from a backup brings everything back – also on a new PC.

Free and Pro

Free: up to 12 recipes, print one recipe at a time as a page or card, Classic and Modern designs. Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): unlimited recipes, the whole cookbook, printing many recipes at once, cookbook sizes 5.5 × 8.5 and 8 × 10 in, and the Farmhouse, Christmas, Tết and Garden designs.
