RecipePress privacy policy

RecipePress works entirely on your own computer. Your recipes and photos are never uploaded, collected or shared. They are kept only on your PC (in the app's own storage) until you delete them. Backup files are saved only where you choose.

The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Purchases of RecipePress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store.
